Lithium vs Lead-Acid

Why switch to lithium?

Litpax Lithium (LFP)

  • ✓Zero maintenance, no water topping
  • ✓Much lighter & compact
  • ✓Fast charging
  • ✓Several times longer life
  • ✓Near full usable capacity
  • ✓No acid, no fumes, eco-friendly

Lead-Acid

  • ✕Regular water topping needed
  • ✕Heavy & bulky
  • ✕Slow charging (8–10 hrs)
  • ✕Frequent replacement
  • ✕~50% usable capacity recommended
  • ✕Acid & lead hazards
